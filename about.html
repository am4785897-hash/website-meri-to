About coder enchante

One workspace for learning and building

coder enchante brings notes,coding,and AI tools into a single connected workspace, so studying and coding don't mean juggling ten different tabs and logins.

It started with a simple problem: preparing for school and learning to code both take real time, and most of that time gets lost switching between apps, searching for the right resource, or rebuilding notes from scratch.coder enchante puts the tools for both — schoolwork and programming — in one place, so more of that time goes into actually learning.

  • 13 tools in one workspace
  • 1 student-built platform
  • 2 focus areas — school & code
